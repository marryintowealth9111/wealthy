## 📌 Project Overview

**Wealthy** is an open-source knowledge repository that decodes the core skills, decision-making frameworks, and wealth philosophies of the world's top 10 billionaires.

Unlike generic success-content, this project integrates **risk management**, **wealth preservation**, and **legacy planning** perspectives—drawing from 13+ years of expertise in financial services and insurance planning.

**What you'll learn:**
- How billionaires think about **risk-adjusted returns**
- The intersection of **entrepreneurship** and **asset protection**
- Strategies for **intergenerational wealth transfer**
- Mindset shifts from "making money" to "keeping and growing money"

## 🎯 Six Pillars of Wealth Mastery

### 1. Wealth Mindset (財富思維)
- First Principles Thinking (第一性原理)
- Long-term vs. Short-term trade-offs
- **Risk-Reward Calibration** (風險回報校準)
- Antifragility: growing stronger from volatility

### 2. Strategic Decision Making (戰略決策)
- Expected Value Calculation
- Asymmetric Risk Opportunities
- **Downside Protection First** (先保護下行風險)
- Opportunity cost analysis

### 3. Time & Energy Capital (時間與能量資本)
- Deep Work & Focus Blocks
- 80/20 Principle (Pareto)
- The Art of Saying "No"
- **Sustainable Performance** (可持續表現)

### 4. Investment & Risk Intelligence (投資與風險智慧)
- Value Investing Principles
- Power of Compounding
- **Asset-Liability Matching** (資產負債匹配)
- Insurance as risk transfer mechanism

### 5. Leadership & Legacy (領導力與傳承)
- Vision Communication
- Talent Acquisition & Retention
- **Family Governance Structures** (家族治理結構)
- Succession Planning

### 6. Innovation & Execution (創新與執行)
- Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
- Rapid Iteration & Pivoting
- **Scalable Systems** (可規模化系統)
- Platform thinking
