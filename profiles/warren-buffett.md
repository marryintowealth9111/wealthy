### 1. Value Investing 價值投資

**Key Concept 核心理念**
- Buy assets priced below intrinsic value
- 買入價格低於內在價值的資產
- Market is a voting machine short-term, weighing machine long-term
- 市場短期是投票機，長期是體重計
- **Margin of Safety** is core protection
- **安全邊際**是核心防護
